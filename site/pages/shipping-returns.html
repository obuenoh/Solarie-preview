Returns

You have 30 days from delivery to return any piece, as long as it is unworn and in its original packaging. Returns are free.

How to return

  1. Email hello@shopsolarie.com with your order number.
  2. We send you a prepaid return label.
  3. Your refund is processed within 5 business days of us receiving the return.

Exchanges
